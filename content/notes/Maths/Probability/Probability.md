`A belief`

A probability is a number between 0 and 1 that we give meaning to.

The probability of an event $E$ is approximately:

$$  
P(E) \approx \frac{\operatorname{count}(E)}{n}  
$$

---

## Conditional Probability

The probability of event $E$ occurring **given that event $F$ has occurred**.

$$  
P(E \mid F) = \frac{P(E \text{ and } F)}{P(F)}  
$$

Using observed frequencies:

$$  
P(E \mid F) \approx \frac{\operatorname{count}(E \text{ and } F)}{\operatorname{count}(F)}  
$$
