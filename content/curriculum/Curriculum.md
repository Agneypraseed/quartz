## ML / AI

### Core machine learning

Main
- [ ] **[Stanford CS229: Machine Learning](https://cs229.stanford.edu/)** — Stanford. Broad foundations, including regression, classification, SVMs, generative models, and introductory deep learning.
- [ ] **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** — Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville. MIT Press textbook with a free official online edition.
---
- [ ] **[Machine Learning — COMS 4771](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~djhsu/coms4771-f25/)** — Daniel Hsu, Columbia. Theory-focused ML, generalization, optimization, and statistical foundations.
- [ ] **[CMU ML Core Curriculum](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/academics/ml-core.html)** — Carnegie Mellon University. Reference for a complete graduate-level core across statistics, optimization, theory, and algorithms.
- [ ] **[Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow, 3rd Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/)** — Aurélien Géron. Practical machine learning and deep learning with widely used Python libraries.
- [ ] **[StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCtYLUTtgS3k1Fg4y5tAhLbw)** — Josh Starmer. Video explanations of statistics, machine learning, neural networks, and AI.

### Build 

- [ ] **[The Spelled-Out Intro to Neural Networks and Backpropagation: Building micrograd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0)** — Andrej Karpathy. A 2 hour 25 minute walkthrough of neural networks and backpropagation.

### Classical AI and decision-making

Main
- [ ] **[Berkeley CS188: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence](https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/fa26/)** — UC Berkeley. Search, constraint satisfaction, game playing, probabilistic inference, Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, and machine learning, with Python projects.

### Generative AI, and multimodal models

Main
- [ ] **[Natural Language Processing with Transformers](https://nlp-with-transformers.github.io/website/)** — Lewis Tunstall, Leandro von Werra, and Thomas Wolf. Establish the NLP and Transformer foundation: tokenization, attention, pretrained models, fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment. The official companion site includes code.
---
- [ ] **[Hands-On Generative AI with Transformers and Diffusion Models](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-generative-ai/9781098149239/)** — Omar Sanseviero, Pedro Cuenca, Apolinário Passos, and Jonathan Whitaker. Extend that foundation to generating text, images, and audio with Transformers and diffusion models.
- [ ] **[Vision Language Models](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/vision-language-models/9798341624030/)** — Merve Noyan, Andrés Marafioti, Miquel Farré, and Orr Zohar. Learn multimodal architectures, image-text alignment, training, fine-tuning, and deployment.
- [ ] **[The Ultra-Scale Playbook: Training LLMs on GPU Clusters](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nanotron/ultrascale-playbook)** — Hugging Face / the Nanotron team. Study GPU memory, compute efficiency, communication, and distributed training after becoming comfortable with model training and Transformer architecture.

### Computer vision and visual intelligence

- [ ] **[EPFL CS-503: Visual Intelligence](https://edu.epfl.ch/coursebook/en/visual-intelligence-CS-503)** — Amir Zamir, EPFL. Classical computer vision, image geometry, correspondence, optical flow, 3D reconstruction, vision Transformers, biological perception, multimodal learning, embodied intelligence, and evaluation.
- [ ] **[CS-503 lecture index](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/course/CS-503)** — EPFL Graph Search. Indexed lecture descriptions, including [image formation and transformation](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/lecture/0_s3kl2hy6) and [neuroscience and visual intelligence](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/lecture/0_88lpdzy9). The index links to recordings that require EPFL login.
- [ ] **[CS-503 Spring 2026 foundation-model homework](https://github.com/EPFL-VILAB/2026-Spring-CS503-Visual-Intelligence-Homework/blob/main/NanoFM_Homeworks/README.md)** — EPFL VILAB. Programming assignments for nanoGPT, nanoMaskGIT, and nano4M: autoregressive generation, masked generation, and multimodal modeling.
- [ ] **[nanoMFM: Multimodal Foundation Models from Scratch](https://github.com/EPFL-VILAB/nanoMFM)** — EPFL VILAB. Educational notebooks, exercise stubs, and reference implementations for nanoGPT, nanoMaskGIT, nano4M, nanoFlow (flow matching), and nanoVLM.
- [ ] **[Vision Science: Photons to Phenomenology](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262161831/vision-science/)** — Stephen E. Palmer. A reference on visual perception combining psychology, computation, and neuroscience; listed in the CS-503 bibliography.
- [ ] **[The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception](https://www.routledge.com/The-Ecological-Approach-to-Visual-Perception-Classic-Edition/Gibson/p/book/9781848725782)** — James J. Gibson. Ecological perception, affordances, and the relationship between vision and action; listed in the CS-503 bibliography.

### Information retrieval

- [ ] **[Introduction to Information Retrieval](https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/)** — Christopher D. Manning, Prabhakar Raghavan, and Hinrich Schütze. A foundation in search, indexing, ranking, and retrieval evaluation. 

### Learning theory

- [ ] **[Machine Learning Theory — COMS 4773](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~djhsu/coms4773-s24/)** — Daniel Hsu, Columbia. Learning theory, sample complexity, PAC-style analysis, and theoretical ML.
- [ ] **[Statistical Learning Theory notes](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~verma/classes/ml/ref/ref_intro_lt.pdf)** — Columbia. Reference notes on VC dimension, generalization bounds, PAC learning, and learning theory.
- [ ] **[The Nature of Statistical Learning Theory](https://statisticalsupportandresearch.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/vladimir-vapnik-the-nature-of-statistical-learning-springer-2010.pdf)** — Vladimir Vapnik. A reference on VC theory, structural risk minimization, and SVM foundations.

### Probabilistic machine learning

- [ ] **[Foundations of Graphical Models course readings](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~blei/fogm/2025F/readings.html)** — David Blei, Columbia. Probabilistic modeling, graphical models, and Bayesian methods.
- [ ] **[Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction and Advanced Topics](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~murphyk/)** — Kevin Murphy. Reference books spanning probability, Bayesian ML, graphical models, deep learning, and modern probabilistic ML.
